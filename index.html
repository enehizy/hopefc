A pathway.

Hope FC Academy trains young footballers at Jeddo Primary School Field. We focus on honest practice, good decisions, and a team that lifts each other up.

The Hope Way

Three things we work on in every session.

  1. 01

    Ball mastery

    First touch, close control and the confidence to want the ball under pressure.

  2. 02

    Game sense

    Scanning, talking on the pitch and picking the right pass at the right time.

  3. 03

    Character

    Discipline, respect and showing up for your teammates, on and off the field.
